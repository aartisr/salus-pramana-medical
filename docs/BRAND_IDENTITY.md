# SALUS Pramana brand identity

## Active logo

The active SALUS Pramana mark represents the platform's promise: compassionate care held to rigorous evidence. It is used in the application header, site favicon, and Apple touch icon.

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Embracing blue forms | Care, protection, and service to patients, caregivers, and clinicians. |
| Four leaf-like panels | The four traditions SALUS brings into one evidence system: Allopathy, Ayurveda, Siddha, and Naturopathy. |
| Central gold point | The shared focus of verified evidence and human wellbeing. |
| Connected points and surrounding arc | Research networks, clinical data, and globally accessible scientific knowledge. |

## Color rationale

- **Navy blue** conveys clinical trust, rigor, and dependable research.
- **Teal** conveys healing, openness, and integrative care.
- **Gold** denotes the high standard of evidence and the human value at the center of the work.

Together, the mark communicates **care held to evidence**: a culturally respectful, research-first bridge across medical systems.
